# Origin Canvas

Start closer to finished with Origin Canvas. Choose from 100 patterns in 18 categories, including 7 full-page designs. Customize your pages in the Site Editor, with no page builder or plugin needed. Use the 12 style variations to change colors, type or both, and Global Styles to adjust the design across your site. The patterns share color, type and spacing presets, so sections line up from page to page. Full demo: https://demo.originblocks.dev/canvas/

These pages describe Canvas 1.5.0.

## What Origin Canvas Includes

Canvas 1.5.0 includes 12 templates, 5 template parts, 100 patterns in 18 categories and 12 style variations. The templates cover pages, posts, the blog, archives, search results and the 404 page. The template parts are the header, footer, sidebar and two mobile menus. The patterns run from a single testimonial card to full home, about, services, pricing and contact pages, and a style variation restyles every one of them at once.

If your site ran Canvas 1.3.1, read [Updating from 1.3.1](getting-started.md#updating-from-131) before you open the Site Editor. The 1.4.0 release renamed the theme's colors, and a site updated in place keeps rendering the same through compatibility styles.
