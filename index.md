## What Origin Canvas Includes

Canvas 1.4.3 includes 12 templates, 5 template parts, 93 patterns in 18 categories and 12 style variations. The templates cover pages, posts, the blog, archives, search results and the 404 page. The template parts are the header, footer, sidebar and two mobile menus. The patterns run from a single testimonial card to full home, about, services, pricing and contact pages, and a style variation restyles every one of them at once.

If your site ran Canvas 1.3.1, read [Updating from 1.3.1](getting-started.md#updating-from-131) before you open the Site Editor. The 1.4.0 release renamed the theme's colors, and a site updated in place keeps rendering the same through compatibility styles.
