# Patterns and Templates

Canvas includes 93 patterns, 12 templates and 5 template parts, so you start from designed sections and site structures instead of building every layout from scratch.

## Patterns

Patterns are page sections and layouts you insert into pages, posts, templates or template parts. Every Canvas pattern uses core blocks and the theme's colors, type and spacing, so sections line up from page to page and a style variation reaches all of them.

The 93 patterns sit in 18 categories of the inserter. Nine are categories WordPress provides: Headers, Footers, Call to action, Text, Testimonials, Team, Posts, Contact and Gallery. Nine are the theme's own: Hero, Pages, Features, Stats, Pricing, Cards, FAQ, Logos and Author.

The Pages category holds seven full pages built from the section patterns: Home: Logos and Cards, Home: Editorial Cover, About: Small on Purpose, Services: A Brand and a Site, Pricing: Plans and FAQ, Contact: Write to the Studio and Landing Page. [Getting Started](getting-started.md) says how to place one.

After inserting a pattern, you can edit its text, images, links, layout and block settings.

## Templates

Templates control page structure by content type. Canvas includes 12: the blog home, a single post, a page, the archive, search results and the 404 page, plus six more a page or post can choose under **Template** in its settings panel.

A page uses **Page (Centered)** unless you choose **Page (Wide)**, **Page (Full Width, No Title)**, **Page (Constrained)**, **Page (Left Sidebar)** or **Page (Right Sidebar)**. A post uses the single template unless you choose **Single (Right Sidebar)**.

## Template Parts

Template parts are sections shared across templates. Canvas has five: Header, Footer, Sidebar, Mobile Menu and Mobile Menu Dark. Every template places the Header and Footer parts, so editing one of them once updates the whole site. The Sidebar part appears in the two sidebar page templates and in **Single (Right Sidebar)**. The two Mobile Menu parts are the navigation overlays on phones, and Mobile Menu Dark pairs with the Header Dark pattern.

Four header patterns (Header, Header Dark, Header Marketing and Header With Button) and three footer patterns (Footer, Footer Dark and Footer Minimal) give the parts a layout to start from. Open the part in the Site Editor and insert one.

## Editing Safely

Before major experiments, duplicate or save a copy of patterns, templates or template parts when appropriate.

This makes it easier to test changes while keeping a known starting point available.
