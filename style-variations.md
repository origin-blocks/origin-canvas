# Style Variations

Canvas includes 12 style variations that change the look of the site without changing its templates. A variation restyles every pattern at once, dark bands and headers included.

## What Style Variations Change

A full variation changes the palette and the typeface. A color preset changes the palette alone, and a typography preset changes the typeface alone.

A variation is a starting point. You can still adjust individual settings after choosing one.

## Choose a Variation

Go to **Appearance > Editor > Styles**.

Browse the available styles, choose the variation that best fits the site, then save the change when you are ready.

## Included Variations

The default style sets Inter on a gray palette with a pink Primary. The other 11 variations are:

- Three full variations, each with its own palette: Editorial sets Source Serif 4 headings, Expressive sets Space Grotesk headings, and Refined Sans sets DM Sans throughout.
- Five color presets: Blue, Green, Orange, Purple and Red.
- Three typography presets: DM Sans, Serif Headings and Space Grotesk.

Use a color preset when you want a different palette. Use a typography preset when you want a different type direction with the same palette.

## After Choosing a Variation

After selecting a variation, you can continue editing individual style settings in the Site Editor.

Those custom changes are saved for your site and override the theme's defaults.
